In recent years, the number of elderly patients who undergo surgery is increasing. Postoperative delirium is a serious complication, and it is said that it occurs in about 20% of patients after cardiovascular surgery. In this study, we examined the effect of a new protocol for the prevention of postoperative delirium in 120 patients who received cardiac surgery in our hospital from April 2022 to March 2024. As a result, the incidence of delirium was 12.5% in the protocol group, and it was 24.1% in the control group, and a significant difference was recognized (p = 0.04). From these results, it was suggested that our protocol is effective for preventing postoperative delirium.

Grammar, articles and usage are corrected and the English made natural. The order and structure of the original are kept as they are.

Postoperative delirium (POD) is a serious complication of cardiovascular surgery, affecting approximately 20% of patients, and its incidence is rising as the surgical population ages. We evaluated a new POD prevention protocol in 120 patients who underwent cardiac surgery at our hospital between April 2022 and March 2024. The incidence of POD was significantly lower in the protocol group than in the control group (12.5% vs. 24.1%; p = 0.04). Our findings suggest that this protocol effectively reduces the incidence of POD after cardiac surgery.

On top of the grammar corrections, the structure is reorganised, the logical flow tightened and the text made more concise, finished to journal style.
